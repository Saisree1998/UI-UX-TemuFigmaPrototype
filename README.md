## Abstract:
Mobile applications have become an integral part of our daily lives, transforming the way we interact with technology. But despite their convenience, users often face frustrating challenges that can hinder their overall experience. One such app that highlights significant flaws in modern app design is **Temu**.

A comprehensive analysis of user feedback reveals numerous usability issues that the app faces. These issues range from intrusive ads to overwhelming notifications and accessibility barriers. These obstacles not only limit user satisfaction but also restrict the app's effectiveness, especially for individuals with disabilities. Therefore, it is crucial to prioritize inclusive app design, emphasizing effective notification management and user-friendly accessibility features.

By addressing these concerns, developers can create a more seamless and enjoyable experience for users of all ages and abilities. This study also emphasizes the importance of tackling common web accessibility barriers found in e-commerce platforms, such as Flash animation and graphic links. By doing so, the app can cater better to individuals with disabilities. Ultimately, prioritizing intuitive notification management and accessible design principles is vital for enhancing the usability and overall user experience of mobile applications like Temu.

---

## Key Focus Areas:

- **Improved Accessibility**: Creating more inclusive design features for users with disabilities.
- **Notification Management**: Redesigning how notifications are managed to avoid user frustration.
- **User Experience**: Overall improvement in usability and satisfaction for all users.
- **Web Accessibility**: Tackling common web accessibility issues, including Flash and graphic links.
